This copy of MeshBench was put here by the Windows installer,
meshbench-<version>-windows-x86_64.msi.

To remove it: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > MeshBench > Uninstall, or
Control Panel > Programs and Features. That removes everything in this
directory and the Start menu entry, and nothing else.

To update it: download the newer .msi from the releases page and run it. It
replaces this installation in place rather than putting a second copy beside
it. Do not unzip a newer build over this directory: the installer's record of
what is here would then describe files that are no longer the ones on disk.

Your own work is not in this directory and an uninstall does not touch it.
Fixtures you have saved, downloaded firmware, terrain tiles and settings live
under your profile, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\meshbench and %APPDATA%\meshbench.

The emulators, radioserver.exe and the shipped fixtures are here beside
meshbench.exe because that is where MeshBench looks for them. Moving
meshbench.exe on its own leaves a build that cannot emulate a board.
