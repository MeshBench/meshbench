# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to get something right.

## The longer version

This project is about measurement, and measurement invites disagreement — about
a number, a method, or whether a result means what somebody says it means. That
disagreement is welcome and is most of the value here. What is not welcome is
making it personal.

**Expected:**

- Argue with the claim, not the person making it.
- Say what you measured and how, so somebody else can check it.
- Accept being wrong gracefully. Everyone here has been, in writing, in this
  repository's history.
- Assume good faith in a report, a question, or a correction.

**Not accepted:**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or dismissing somebody's contribution
  because of who they are rather than what they said.
- Publishing anyone's private information.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages and any other space
where you are acting as a participant in this project.

## Enforcement

Report to **meshbench@pm.me**. Reports are read by the maintainer and
kept confidential.

There is one maintainer, so this is not a committee process: expect a
conversation first, and a removal of the ability to participate if the
behaviour continues or was severe enough not to warrant a first warning.
